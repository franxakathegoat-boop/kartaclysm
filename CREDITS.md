## Cup icons (pack/build/cup-icons.png)

Made for Kartaclysm. The disaster symbols are from Google's **Material Symbols** (Apache License 2.0, https://github.com/google/material-design-icons); text in Chakra Petch (OFL). Retro Rewind's cup icons inspired the idea of a symbol on every cup, but none of their files are used (we have no license to redistribute them).

## Title screen and music

- In-game title screen (pack/build/title): drawn procedurally for Kartaclysm (assets-src/title/title.py), with the Kartaclysm logo. No Nintendo or Retro Rewind art.
- "Kartaclysm Theme" (launcher music, src/Assets/launcher_music.wav): original composition generated by assets-src/music/music.py for this project. Released as CC0 (public domain).

## Unlock everything

"Unlock Everything Without Save" by _tZ (https://mariokartwii.com/showthread.php?tid=1736), PAL address 0x80549974 - the same patch Pulsar ships (PulsarSystem.cpp). Written directly into StaticR.rel by prepare.ps1 (one instruction), no cheat handler. The save file is never modified.

Original Kartaclysm symbol and wordmark, generated by art4/logo4.py.
